Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about crafting digital moments.

How do I publish a wish?
Once you have generated your page, click the "Publish Online" button in the top right corner. You can optionally protect it with a password. You will instantly receive a live link to share!
Can I edit a wish after publishing?
Yes! Go to "My Wishes Dashboard" in the menu. Click "✎ Edit Code" to load the page back into the builder. Once you finish your edits, simply publish it again.
What is an Interactive Vault?
Vaults are cinematic lock screens that build anticipation. Before the recipient can read your letter, they might have to "scan" their thumbprint, scratch off a digital lottery ticket, or enter a passcode.
Where do I get a Redeem Code?
Premium redeem codes are given out during promotional events, on our social media channels, or directly via developer support. They unlock Artisan or Studio tier features instantly!

API Documentation

Automate your gift generation. Requires Studio Tier.

POST /v1/wishes/generate

Programmatically generate a completely new WishCraft page by sending JSON intake parameters to our secure endpoint.

Example Request
fetch('https://api.wishcraft.app/v1/wishes/generate', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: {
    'Authorization': 'Bearer wc_live_YOUR_KEY',
    'Content-Type': 'application/json'
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    "recipientName": "Alex",
    "themeName": "midnight-arcade",
    "complexity": "interactive-vault",
    "features": ["Confetti Finale", "Love Meter"]
  })
});
Authentication

API keys can be generated from the Admin dashboard once your account is upgraded to the Studio tier. Never share your secret key in client-side code.
